## 1. CELL BIOLOGY

### 1.1 Prokaryote vs Eukaryote

| Feature | Prokaryote | Eukaryote |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | Absent (nucleoid region) | Present (membrane-bound) |
| Size | 1–10 µm | 10–100 µm |
| DNA | Circular, no histones | Linear, with histones |
| Membrane-bound organelles | Absent | Present |
| Ribosomes | 70S (50S + 30S) | 80S (60S + 40S) |
| Cell wall | Present (peptidoglycan in bacteria) | Present in plants (cellulose), absent in animals |
| Examples | Bacteria, Archaea, Cyanobacteria | Plants, Animals, Fungi, Protists |
| Reproduction | Binary fission | Mitosis / Meiosis |

> ⚠️ **TRAP:** Mitochondria & chloroplasts have 70S ribosomes (like prokaryotes) — supports endosymbiotic theory!

### 1.2 Cell Organelles

| Organelle | Function | Membrane-Bound? |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | DNA storage, gene expression control | Yes (double) |
| Mitochondria | ATP production (cellular respiration) — "powerhouse" | Yes (double) |
| Chloroplast | Photosynthesis (plants only) | Yes (double) |
| Endoplasmic Reticulum (Rough) | Protein synthesis (has ribosomes) | Yes |
| Endoplasmic Reticulum (Smooth) | Lipid synthesis, detoxification | Yes |
| Golgi Apparatus | Protein modification, packaging, secretion — "post office" | Yes |
| Lysosome | Intracellular digestion, autophagy — "suicide bags" | Yes |
| Ribosome | Protein synthesis | No |
| Vacuole | Storage (large in plants — tonoplast membrane) | Yes |

> 🔑 **MUST REMEMBER:**
> - Mitochondria = Double membrane → Powerhouse
> - Lysosome = "Suicide bags" (self-destruction in autolysis)
> - Ribosome = NOT membrane-bound (despite being in RER)
> - Golgi = Processes proteins from RER → sends to lysosomes or cell surface

### 1.3 Cell Division

#### MITOSIS (Somatic cells — growth & repair)

```
Interphase → Prophase → Metaphase → Anaphase → Telophase → Cytokinesis
```

| Phase | Key Events |
|---|---|
| **Interphase (G1,S,G2)** | Cell grows; DNA replicates in S phase |
| **Prophase** | Chromatin condenses into chromosomes; spindle forms; nuclear envelope breaks |
| **Metaphase** | Chromosomes align at equatorial plate (metaphase plate) |
| **Anaphase** | Sister chromatids pulled to opposite poles by spindle fibres |
| **Telophase** | Nuclear envelopes reform; chromosomes decondense |
| **Cytokinesis** | Cytoplasm divides → 2 identical diploid daughter cells |

**Result:** 2 diploid (2n) cells genetically identical to parent

#### MEIOSIS (Reproductive cells — gamete formation)

```
Meiosis I (Reductional division) → Meiosis II (Equational division)
```

| Phase | Key Events |
|---|---|
| **Prophase I** | Homologous chromosomes pair (synapsis); crossing over (chiasmata) → genetic recombination |
| **Metaphase I** | Homologous pairs align at equatorial plate |
| **Anaphase I** | Homologous chromosomes separate (NOT sister chromatids) |
| **Telophase I** | 2 haploid cells formed |
| **Meiosis II** | Like mitosis — sister chromatids separate |
| **Result** | 4 haploid (n) cells — genetically diverse |

> ⚠️ **TRAP:** In Anaphase I of meiosis, homologous chromosomes separate (not sister chromatids — that's Anaphase II and mitotic Anaphase).

## 3. BLOOD

### 3.1 Blood Components

| Component | Normal Count | Lifespan | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RBC (Erythrocytes)** | 4.5–5.5 million/µL (M); 4–5 million/µL (F) | 120 days | Carry O₂ via haemoglobin; CO₂ transport |
| **WBC (Leukocytes)** | 4,000–11,000/µL | Days to weeks | Immunity; fight infection |
| **Platelets (Thrombocytes)** | 1.5–4 lakh/µL | 8–10 days | Blood clotting (coagulation) |
| **Plasma** | ~55% of blood volume | — | Transport nutrients, hormones, waste; contains clotting factors |

### WBC Types (NLEMBM)
- **Neutrophils** – 60–70% – First responders to infection (phagocytosis)
- **Lymphocytes** – 20–25% – B cells (antibodies), T cells (cellular immunity)
- **Monocytes** – 2–6% – Become macrophages; phagocytosis
- **Eosinophils** – 1–4% – Allergic response, anti-parasitic
- **Basophils** – 0.5–1% – Release histamine (inflammation)

> **Mnemonic (Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas):** Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, Basophils

### 3.2 ABO Blood Groups

| Blood Group | Antigen on RBC | Antibody in Plasma | Can Donate To | Can Receive From |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | A | Anti-B | A, AB | A, O |
| **B** | B | Anti-A | B, AB | B, O |
| **AB** | A and B | None | AB only | A, B, AB, O (**Universal Recipient**) |
| **O** | None | Anti-A and Anti-B | A, B, AB, O (**Universal Donor**) | O only |

### Rh Factor
- **Rh+** = Rh antigen present (85% of humans)
- **Rh–** = Rh antigen absent
- **Erythroblastosis foetalis:** Rh– mother carries Rh+ baby → mother produces anti-Rh antibodies → danger in second pregnancy
- **Prevention:** RhoGAM (anti-D immunoglobulin) injection

### 3.3 Blood Clotting
```
Injury → Platelet plug → Prothrombin → Thrombin → Fibrinogen → Fibrin mesh → Clot
```
- **Vitamin K** is essential for synthesis of clotting factors (II, VII, IX, X)
- **Heparin** = natural anticoagulant (produced by liver, mast cells)
- **Haemophilia** = genetic disorder; deficiency of Factor VIII (Haemophilia A) or Factor IX (Haemophilia B)

## 6. DNA & GENETICS

### 6.1 DNA Structure
- **Double helix** (Watson & Crick, 1953; X-ray data by Rosalind Franklin)
- **Nucleotide** = Phosphate + Deoxyribose sugar + Nitrogenous base
- **Bases:** Purines (Adenine, Guanine) | Pyrimidines (Thymine, Cytosine)
- **Base pairs:** A=T (2 hydrogen bonds) | G≡C (3 hydrogen bonds)
- **Anti-parallel strands:** 5'→3' and 3'→5'

```
5'—A—T—G—C—A—3'
   | | | | |
3'—T—A—C—G—T—5'
```

### 6.2 Central Dogma of Molecular Biology
```
DNA → (Replication) → DNA
DNA → (Transcription) → mRNA → (Translation) → Protein
```

| Process | Location | Key Enzyme | Product |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Replication** | Nucleus | DNA Polymerase | Copy of DNA |
| **Transcription** | Nucleus | RNA Polymerase | mRNA |
| **Translation** | Ribosome | Ribosome (rRNA + proteins) | Protein |

> ⚠️ **TRAP:** In prokaryotes, transcription and translation occur simultaneously in cytoplasm (no nuclear membrane). In eukaryotes, transcription → nucleus; translation → cytoplasm.

### 6.3 Mendel's Laws

| Law | Statement |
|---|---|
| **Law of Segregation** | Each organism has two alleles for each trait; they separate during gamete formation |
| **Law of Independent Assortment** | Genes for different traits are inherited independently of each other (when on different chromosomes) |
| **Law of Dominance** | In a cross between pure lines, only the dominant trait appears in F1 |

**Monohybrid Cross (Tt × Tt):**
```
Tt × Tt → TT : 2Tt : tt = 3 Tall : 1 Short (Phenotype 3:1; Genotype 1:2:1)
```

**Dihybrid Cross (TtRr × TtRr):** → 9:3:3:1 phenotypic ratio

### 6.4 Blood Group Genetics

| Alleles | Blood Group |
|---|---|
| I^A I^A or I^A i | A |
| I^B I^B or I^B i | B |
| I^A I^B | AB (codominance) |
| ii | O |

> Blood group AB demonstrates **codominance** (both alleles expressed equally)
> Blood group O demonstrates **recessive** inheritance

## 9. PLANT BIOLOGY

### 9.1 Photosynthesis
```
6CO₂ + 6H₂O + Light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
(occurs in chloroplast — specifically thylakoids for light reactions, stroma for dark reactions)
```

| Stage | Location | Reactants | Products |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Light Reactions** | Thylakoid membrane | H₂O, Light | ATP, NADPH, O₂ (released) |
| **Calvin Cycle (Dark)** | Stroma | CO₂, ATP, NADPH | Glucose (G3P) |

### 9.2 Cellular Respiration
```
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 36–38 ATP
```

| Stage | Location | Product |
|---|---|---|
| Glycolysis | Cytoplasm | 2 ATP, 2 Pyruvate |
| Krebs Cycle | Mitochondria matrix | CO₂, NADH, FADH₂ |
| Electron Transport Chain | Inner mitochondrial membrane | 34 ATP, H₂O |

### 9.3 Plant Hormones

| Hormone | Function | Practical Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Auxins (IAA)** | Cell elongation; apical dominance; root initiation | Rooting powder; weedkiller |
| **Gibberellins** | Stem elongation; seed germination; breaks dormancy | Brewing industry (barley germination) |
| **Cytokinins** | Cell division; delays senescence; shoot development | Tissue culture |
| **Abscisic Acid (ABA)** | Stress hormone; closes stomata; seed dormancy | — |
| **Ethylene (Ethene)** | Fruit ripening; leaf abscission; "aging hormone" | Ripening bananas artificially |

### 9.4 Tropisms

| Tropism | Stimulus | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Phototropism | Light | Shoots grow towards (positive); roots away (negative) |
| Geotropism (Gravitropism) | Gravity | Roots grow towards (positive); shoots away (negative) |
| Hydrotropism | Water | Roots grow towards water |
| Thigmotropism | Touch | Tendrils coil around support |
| Chemotropism | Chemicals | Pollen tube grows towards ovule |

### 9.5 Transpiration
- Loss of water vapour from aerial parts of plant (mainly through stomata)
- **Guard cells** control stomatal opening (open in light, close in dark/drought)
- **Transpiration pull** = main force for water movement up xylem (cohesion-tension theory)

## 10. ECOLOGY

### 10.1 Food Chain & Food Web
```
Producers (Plants) → Primary consumers (Herbivores) → Secondary consumers (Carnivores) → Tertiary consumers
                                                                                           ↑
                                                                                     Decomposers (Bacteria, Fungi)
```

### 10.2 Trophic Levels & Energy Flow
- **10% Law (Lindeman):** Only 10% of energy transfers from one trophic level to the next
- Energy lost as: heat, respiration, excretion

### 10.3 Ecological Pyramids

| Pyramid | What it shows | Can be inverted? |
|---|---|---|
| **Pyramid of Numbers** | Count of organisms at each trophic level | Yes (e.g., one oak tree → many insects) |
| **Pyramid of Biomass** | Total dry weight at each trophic level | Yes (aquatic ecosystems — phytoplankton vs zooplankton) |
| **Pyramid of Energy** | Energy content at each trophic level | Never inverted (always upright) |

### 10.4 Biodiversity

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| **Genetic Diversity** | Variation within species (different alleles) |
| **Species Diversity** | Variety of species in an area |
| **Ecosystem Diversity** | Variety of ecosystems/habitats |

**Hotspots of Biodiversity:** Areas with ≥1500 endemic vascular plants AND ≥70% habitat lost
- India's hotspots: **Western Ghats, Eastern Himalayas, Indo-Burma, Sundaland**

### 10.5 Endangered Species Categories (IUCN Red List)
- **Extinct (EX)** → **Extinct in Wild (EW)** → **Critically Endangered (CR)** → **Endangered (EN)** → **Vulnerable (VU)** → **Near Threatened (NT)** → **Least Concern (LC)**

**India's Critical Species:** Bengal Tiger, Indian Rhino, Snow Leopard, Asiatic Lion (Gir), Gangetic Dolphin (National Aquatic Animal)

## 11. BIOTECHNOLOGY

### 11.1 PCR (Polymerase Chain Reaction)
- **Purpose:** Amplify specific DNA segments in vitro (billions of copies from tiny sample)
- **Steps:** Denaturation (94°C) → Annealing (50–65°C, primers attach) → Extension (72°C, DNA Polymerase — Taq polymerase)
- **Invented by:** Kary Mullis (Nobel Prize 1993)
- **Uses:** DNA fingerprinting, diagnosis, forensics, COVID testing

### 11.2 Gel Electrophoresis
- Separates DNA fragments by **size** using electric field
- DNA is negatively charged → moves toward positive electrode (anode)
- Smaller fragments travel farther (through agarose gel pores)
- Visualized with ethidium bromide staining under UV light

### 11.3 Recombinant DNA Technology
```
Cut DNA with Restriction Enzymes → Insert gene into Vector (plasmid) → 
Transform into host cell (bacteria) → Clone and express protein
```
- **Restriction enzymes** = "molecular scissors" that cut DNA at specific sequences
- **Vectors:** Plasmids, Bacteriophages, BAC, YAC
- **Example:** Human insulin produced by *E. coli* (Humulin)

### 11.4 GM Crops (Genetically Modified)

| Crop | Modification | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| **Bt cotton** | *Bacillus thuringiensis* toxin gene | Resistant to bollworm pest |
| **Golden Rice** | β-carotene (Vitamin A) biosynthesis | Vitamin A deficiency prevention |
| **Bt Brinjal** | Bt toxin | Insect resistance |
| **Flavr Savr Tomato** | Antisense gene for polygalacturonase | Delayed ripening |

### 11.5 Stem Cells
- **Totipotent:** Can form entire organism (fertilized egg)
- **Pluripotent:** Can form all body cell types (embryonic stem cells)
- **Multipotent:** Can form limited cell types (adult stem cells)

### 11.6 CRISPR-Cas9
- **Full form:** Clustered Regularly Interspaced Short Palindromic Repeats
- **Function:** Precise gene editing — guide RNA directs Cas9 nuclease to cut specific DNA sequence
- **Nobel Prize 2020:** Jennifer Doudna and Emmanuelle Charpentier
