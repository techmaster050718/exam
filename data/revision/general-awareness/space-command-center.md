### Space facts
- ISRO is the principal civilian space agency of India.
- Launch vehicles are used to place satellites into orbit.
- India has a strong space programme in communications, navigation, remote sensing, and weather monitoring.
- Space capabilities have both civilian and strategic dual-use relevance.

### Why space matters in national security
- Communications resilience and disaster response
- Navigation and timing support for transport, defence, and governance
- Earth observation for border monitoring and natural disasters
- Strategic positioning in a contested geopolitical environment
- Growing commercialisation and private-sector participation

## 2. Launch vehicles

### PSLV
- Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle.
- Famous for reliable launches of remote sensing and other satellites.
- Used for many Indian and international satellite missions.

### GSLV and LVM3
- GSLV is the geostationary launch vehicle programme; LVM3 is the heavy-lift launcher used in later phases.
- Important for heavier satellites and strategic payloads.

### Why it matters
- Launch capability is central to India’s space autonomy and strategic reach.

## 4. Major missions and programmes

### Chandrayaan
- India’s lunar exploration programme.
- Important for science and technology capability.

### Mangalyaan
- Mars Orbiter Mission.
- Demonstrated India’s capability in interplanetary mission design and operations.

### Aditya-L1
- Solar observation mission.
- Key for studying the Sun and solar activity.

### Gaganyaan
- India’s human spaceflight programme.
- Represents a major ambition in human space exploration.

### Strategic space applications
- Communication resilience
- Disaster monitoring
- Surveillance and reconnaissance
- Navigation and timing
- Space situational awareness

## 5. Defence and strategic relevance

- Space assets are integral to communications, weather monitoring, and reconnaissance.
- Navigation and timing support military and civilian infrastructure.

- Space platforms are dual-use, serving both civilian and security needs.

## 7. Common exam traps

- ISRO is not a military agency; it is the civilian space organisation.
- Space technology has strategic applications, but public exams should stick to officially known facts.
- PSLV, GSLV, and LVM3 are launch systems, not satellites.
- NavIC, INSAT, and GSAT serve different purposes despite being space-related.
