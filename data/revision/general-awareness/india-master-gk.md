### Strategic geography snapshot
- Himalayan states shape the northern land frontier and include major passes.
- The north-west is strategically important for border security, mountain routes, and western river systems.
- The eastern and northeastern borders are sensitive for terrain, biodiversity, and cross-border connectivity.
- The peninsular coast remains critical for trade, naval logistics, and maritime security.
- India’s island territories strengthen surveillance and maritime reach in the Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea.

## 11. Common exam traps

- State vs Union Territory: do not confuse them.
- National language vs official language: not the same concept.
- Himalayan region is not the only strategic geography; maritime and peninsular domains matter too.
- India’s geography is diversified and includes mountains, plains, plateau, coasts, islands and desert.
- Article numbers and basic constitutional concepts are common exam questions.
