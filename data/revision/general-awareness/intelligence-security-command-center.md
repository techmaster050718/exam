## 2. RAW — Publicly known facts

### RAW
- Full form: Research and Analysis Wing.
- Established in 1968, after the 1962 and 1965 war context prompted a review of intelligence organisation.
- Broad mandate: external intelligence and strategic assessment.
- Administrative context: part of the national security structure and directly linked to the government’s strategic intelligence architecture.
- Distinction: RAW is not the same as IB or DIA.

### RAW vs IB vs DIA vs NTRO vs MAC
| Agency | Broad domain | Main function |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| RAW | External intelligence | Foreign intelligence and strategic assessment |
| IB | Internal/domestic | Internal security and coordination |
| DIA | Defence/military intelligence | Military and operational assessment |
| NTRO | Technical security and national intelligence infrastructure | Technical and communications-related intelligence support |
| MAC | Military assessment / strategic intelligence context | Defence and force-level assessment within the military ecosystem |

### Strategic intelligence architecture (exam view)
- RAW is primarily associated with external/foreign intelligence.
- IB is primarily associated with domestic/internal intelligence.
- DIA and military intelligence structures focus on defence and force-level assessment.
- NTRO adds technical and national-security infrastructure intelligence support.
- These agencies are complementary but distinct; their mandate is not interchangeable.

### Publicly known vs classified
Operational details, agent networks, and covert methods are classified or not publicly established. These must be explicitly described as such rather than discussed as factual details.

## 3. Intelligence Bureau (IB)

- IB is India’s domestic/internal intelligence agency.
- Broad role includes internal security intelligence, counter-intelligence, and coordination with security structures.
- It operates in a domestic security ecosystem alongside state police, CAPFs, and other central agencies.
- The distinction from RAW is important: different broad domains and responsibilities.

### IB and RAW: exam comparison
- IB: Indian domestic intelligence and internal security coordination.
- RAW: external intelligence and foreign assessment.

### Executive and security linkage
The intelligence ecosystem sits alongside the police, military, and administrative structures. In public understanding, the focus of questions is usually on:
- what the agency does,
- which domain it serves,
- whether it is domestic, external, military, or technical intelligence,
- and which ministry or command structure it is linked to.
The underlying covert functioning remains classified and outside standard exam factual treatment.

## 4. NIA, CBI, NSG and related agencies

### Investigative and response agencies matrix
| Agency | Main role | Broad distinction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| NIA | National investigation into specified terrorism and national-security offences | Investigative, not a military formation |
| CBI | Central investigation of serious offences | Broad federal investigative mandate |
| NSG | Counter-terror response and specialized tactical operations | Specialized security response, not a regular armed force |
| ED | Economic and financial investigation | Specialised financial enforcement |
| NCRB | Crime data and records | Statistical and administrative intelligence support |
| BPR&D | Police modernization and research | Institutional support, not frontline intelligence |

### NIA
- National Investigation Agency.
- Mandate: investigation of offences under specific national security, terror, and cross-border threat categories.
- It works in a coordinated framework with state police and central agencies.

### CBI
- Central Bureau of Investigation.
- Broad role: central investigation of serious federal and inter-state crime matters.
- Distinct from NIA in terms of broad mandate and investigative focus.

### NSG
- National Security Guard.
- Elite anti-terror and counter-terror response force.
- Distinct from Army Para SF and from the Nuclear Suppliers Group.

### Other important security agencies
- ED: Enforcement Directorate.
- NCB: Narcotics Control Bureau.
- DRI: Directorate of Revenue Intelligence.
- NCRB: National Crime Records Bureau.
- MAC: Military intelligence and strategic assessment in the defence ecosystem.
- BPR&D: Bureau of Police Research and Development.

## 5. CAPFs and police intelligence linkages

- BSF, CRPF, ITBP, CISF, SSB, Assam Rifles are central armed police forces.
- Their role includes border management, internal security, law and order support, and specialized deployments.
- State police units, ATS, STF, Special Branch, and cybercrime units work alongside central agencies.

### Why this matters
Exam questions often test agency distinction and broad domain matching rather than operational details.

### Security matrix: intelligence vs response vs investigation
| Function | Typical agencies | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| External intelligence | RAW | Foreign-threat assessment, strategic intelligence |
| Internal intelligence | IB, state intelligence units | Domestic security, threats and coordination |
| Military intelligence | DIA, MAC | Operational and strategic military assessment |
| Technical intelligence | NTRO | Technical and communications-related support |
| Investigation | NIA, CBI, ED, state police | Evidence gathering and prosecution support |
| Response / tactical action | NSG, CAPFs, state police | Intervention in emergencies and high-risk incidents |

## 6. Security architecture and internal security

India’s internal security matrix includes:
- Central agencies (IB, NIA, NSG, CAPFs)
- State police and law-enforcement agencies
- Special units such as ATS and STF
- Intelligence coordination and anti-terror frameworks
- Cybersecurity and digital surveillance coordination in the public-sector framework

### Internal security challenges
- Terrorism and radicalisation
- Left-wing extremism
- Cross-border infiltration and border management
- Cybercrime and digital fraud
- Organized crime and trafficking
- Critical infrastructure protection

## 7. Common confusion traps

- RAW vs IB: external vs domestic intelligence.
- NSG (National Security Guard) vs NSG (Nuclear Suppliers Group).
- NIA vs CBI: national security-driven investigation vs broader central investigation.
- NTRO vs RAW: technical intelligence support vs external intelligence agency.
- CAPF vs Armed Forces: separate, though sometimes overlapping in security roles.
