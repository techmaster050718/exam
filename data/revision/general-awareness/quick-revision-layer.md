### One-liners
- India has 28 states and 8 UTs.
- Kargil was the key high-altitude conflict in the 1999 war.
- Para SF is the Army’s elite special operations unit.
- MARCOS are the Navy’s special maritime operators.
- ISRO is the main civilian space agency.
- PSLV is a major Indian launch vehicle.
- DAE, BARC, and NPCIL are key civilian nuclear institutions.
- CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, and Assam Rifles are major CAPFs.
- BRICS, QUAD, BIMSTEC, and SCO are major strategic groupings.

- NTRO = National Technical Research Organisation.
