## 1. Top 100 India GK facts

### Exam memory map
| Pillar | Core recall points |
| :--- | :--- |
| Geography | Himalayas, Indo-Gangetic plains, peninsular plateau, coasts, rivers |
| Constitution & polity | Preamble, Parliament, federalism, judiciary, states/UTs |

| Economy | Agriculture, industry, logistics, infrastructure, energy |

- India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.
- New Delhi is the National Capital Territory and seat of government.
- Constitution came into effect on 26 January 1950.
- India is the world’s largest democracy.
- The Himalayas, Deccan Plateau, Indo-Gangetic Plain, and coastal plains are core physical divisions.
- Ganga, Brahmaputra, Godavari, Krishna, and Narmada are major river systems.
- Kanyakumari is the southern tip of mainland India.
- Siachen Glacier is in the Himalayas.
- India has a highly diverse climate and biodiversity.
- UPSC is the central recruitment body for civil services.

## 2. Top 100 defence facts

- India has three services: Army, Navy, and Air Force.
- Chief of Defence Staff coordinates jointness at strategic level.
- CDS is a major military reform and command relationship concept.
- Para SF is the Indian Army’s elite special operations unit.
- MARCOS are naval special operations personnel.
- Garud is the IAF special force.
- DRDO handles defence research and development.
- BrahMos, Akash, and Agni are prominent public defence systems.
- The Ministry of Defence oversees the armed forces.
- Operation Vijay (Goa) and Kargil are major historical operations.

## 3. Top 100 IR and security facts

- UN headquarters is in New York.
- IMF headquarters is in Washington, D.C.
- ASEAN is headquartered in Jakarta.
- SAARC headquarters is in Kathmandu.
- BIMSTEC brings together countries around the Bay of Bengal.
- BRICS is a major emerging economies grouping.
- QUAD is a strategic partnership among India, Japan, Australia, and the US.
- Indo-Pacific is the key strategic theatre for India.
- Liaoning, Malabar, and other maritime exercises show maritime cooperation.
- Intelligence agencies are distinct from police and military organisations.

## 4. Top 100 police and CAPF facts

- DGP is the head of a state police force.
- Police hierarchy moves from constable to DGP.
- CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, and Assam Rifles are major CAPFs.
- BSF guards the India–Pakistan and India–Bangladesh borders.
- ITBP is deployed along the India–China border.
- CISF guards critical infrastructure and airports.
- State police handle law and order and local policing functions.
- Police and intelligence agencies differ in mandate and structure.
