## 2. Operation Vijay (Goa, 1961)

- Operation: Goa liberation
- Year: 1961
- Location/Theatre: Goa, Daman and Diu
- Primary Service: Indian Army, Navy, Air Force
- Objective: Liberate Portuguese territories in India
- Background: Portuguese rule in Goa continued after Indian independence and ended through a military operation.
- Major events: Indian armed forces advanced rapidly; diplomatic pressure and military action culminated in surrender.
- Outcome: Goa integrated into India.
- Strategic significance: symbolized post-independence territorial consolidation.
- Exam facts: 1961, Goa, Portuguese rule, liberation.
- Common confusion: Do not confuse Goa liberation with later naval or air operations.
- Verification: public records, official history, government sources.

## 3. 1947–48 Jammu & Kashmir operations

- Operation: Jammu & Kashmir military operations during accession crisis
- Year: 1947–48
- Location/Theatre: J&K
- Primary Service: Indian Army
- Objective: Secure the state after tribal invasion and the accession process
- Background: J&K accession to India and subsequent military intervention
- Major events: early battles, relief operations, Valley defense, strategic encirclement and advancing forces
- Outcome: India retained control of a major part of the state and established a military front that shaped the later ceasefire line
- Strategic significance: first major post-independence war and a defining security challenge
- Exam facts: 1947–48 war, J&K, accession, ceasefire line context
- Common confusion: Do not overstate tactical details or the exact lines without official reporting.
- Verification: official histories and public military accounts

## 4. 1965 India-Pakistan War

- Operation: 1965 War
- Year: 1965
- Location/Theatre: Western front, Kashmir sector, Punjab, Rajasthan sectors
- Primary Service: Army, Air Force, Navy
- Objective: Reassert strategic control and respond to cross-border action
- Background: erupted after the Rann of Kutch episode and escalating tensions over Kashmir
- Major events: operations in the Chhamb sector, Lahore front, air battles, strategic artillery operations
- Outcome: war ended without a decisive territorial change, but concluded with a ceasefire and strategic military lessons
- Strategic significance: highlighted air power, logistics, and operational planning
- Exam facts: 1965 war, Operation Grand Slam, Lahore front, war in the western sector
- Common confusion: do not confuse it with the 1947–48 war or the 1971 war
- Verification: official war histories and public records

## 5. 1971 Bangladesh Liberation War

- Operation: Liberation War / Operation Searchlight (Pakistan side) / Indian military intervention
- Year: 1971
- Location/Theatre: Eastern theatre, Bangladesh, western front
- Primary Service: Indian Army, Navy, Air Force
- Objective: Support the Bangladesh liberation movement and defeat Pakistan forces in the East
- Background: East Pakistan crisis, refugee influx, and military action by Pakistan forces
- Major events: Mukti Bahini cooperation, eastern theatre operations, Operation Trident (naval), Operation Python (naval), air operations, Battle of Longewala in the western theatre, rapid ground advance
- Outcome: Pakistan surrendered in East Pakistan; Bangladesh came into existence
- Strategic significance: watershed in Indian military history; major demonstration of joint operations and strategic planning
- Exam facts: 1971 war, East Pakistan, Bangladesh, Mukti Bahini, Operation Trident, Operation Python, Battle of Longewala
- Common confusion: battle of Longewala belongs to the western theatre and should not be confused with eastern operations
- Verification: official histories, declassified public accounts, and widely documented war records

## 11. Operation Vijay (Kargil, 1999)

- Operation: Vijay
- Year: 1999
- Location/Theatre: Kargil sector, Eastern Ladakh
- Primary Service: Indian Army, Air Force, supporting Army and Air support
- Objective: Evict Pakistani intruders from positions in the Kargil sector
- Background: infiltration and occupation of mountain positions in the Kargil region
- Major events: Operation Safed Sagar (IAF air strikes), artillery and infantry actions, mountain warfare, tactical recapture of positions
- Outcome: India regained strategic positions and established the importance of high-altitude combat and air support coordination
- Strategic significance: one of the defining modern Indian military operations
- Exam facts: Kargil 1999, Operation Vijay, Operation Safed Sagar, mountain warfare, high-altitude operations
- Common confusion: Operation Vijay is not the same as Operation Vijay (Goa, 1961)
- Verification: official and public military documentation

## 25. Common exam traps

- Operation Vijay 1961 vs Operation Vijay 1999.
- Battle of Longewala is not the entire 1971 war; it was a major western front event.
- Operation Trident and Operation Python were naval operations; do not confuse them with any air strike or army operation.
- Operation Meghdoot is in Siachen, not near a coastline.
- Surgical strikes and Balakot are not interchangeable.
