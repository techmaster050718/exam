## 2. Indigenous missile and air-defence systems

### Major public examples
- BrahMos: supersonic cruise missile system.
- Akash: surface-to-air missile system.
- Agni: long-range missile family.
- Prithvi: tactical ballistic missile family.
- Barak-8: medium-range surface-to-air missile system.

### Why they matter
- They reflect India’s strategic and defence-industrial capabilities.
- They are often used in exam questions on indigenous defence systems.

---

## 3. Aircraft, helicopters, and drones

- Fighter aircraft and transport aircraft remain central to operational readiness.
- Helicopters support mobility, rescue, air assault, and reconnaissance.
- UAVs and drones are used for ISR, surveillance, and tactical support.
- The Indian armed forces have expanded indigenous and imported platform capacity across aviation, surveillance, and support missions.

### Exam angle
- Always check the distinction between aircraft platforms and missile or air-defence systems.

---

## 4. Naval and maritime technologies

- Destroyers, frigates, corvettes, submarines, and naval aviation are major components of maritime deterrence.
- Sonar, radars, anti-submarine warfare, and naval surveillance are core maritime security technologies.
- Coastal and littoral security rely on combination of naval platforms, surveillance, and coast guard coordination.

---

## 5. Electronic warfare, radars, and communications

- EW includes jamming, deception, signal intelligence, and electronic countermeasures.
- Radars provide surveillance, tracking, and air defence support.
- Secure communication systems are critical for command and control.
- Modern battlefield awareness depends on secure networks and ISR integration.

## 8. Common exam traps

- Missile systems are not the same as aircraft systems.
- Air defence is different from air force combat aviation.
- Drones and UAVs are part of modern warfare, but public details must remain non-sensitive.
- Indigenous defence capability is a broad theme; avoid unsupported technical claims.

## 9. Source and verification

Use official DRDO, Ministry of Defence, HAL, BEL, public military press releases, and official defence procurement/documentation. Avoid sensitive technical details not publicly disclosed.
