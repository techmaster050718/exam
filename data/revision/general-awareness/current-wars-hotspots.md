### STATIC BACKGROUND
Static background is meant to cover broad historical, geographic, strategic, and institutional context that remains useful across multiple editions.

### CURRENT STATUS
Current status is meant to present the latest publicly visible trend, crisis, or operational posture without turning dynamic reporting into permanent fact.

## 5. Dynamic field notes

### Rule: always date-stamp sensitive information
Bad: “The war is ongoing.”
Better: “As of 15 Nov 2026, reports indicated continuing hostilities in the region.”

### Static revision linking
- Ukraine → NATO, Black Sea, Europe, sanctions, energy security.
- Gaza / Israel → Middle East, maritime routes, energy, diplomacy, humanitarian law.
- Red Sea → Bab-el-Mandeb, maritime security, Indian Ocean, shipping.
- India–China LX border → LAC, Galwan, infrastructure, high-altitude warfare.
- AI or semiconductor issues → technology, cyber, geopolitics, industrial strategy.

---

## 6. Quick exam revision

- Current affairs are dynamic and date-sensitive.
- Static GK and current affairs are linked but must remain separate.
- For competition prep, examine major geopolitical impact, not just rhetoric.
- Know the actors, location, and why it matters.

---

## 7. Source and verification

Current conflicts and geopolitical hotspot information must use official statements, reliable reporting, and public government releases. If uncertain, label as developing or under public verification rather than making a definitive claim.
